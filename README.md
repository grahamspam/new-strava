This project addresses a gap in existing fitness tracking tools for experienced runners who need comprehensive training insights beyond basic GPS data. 
While apps like Strava excel at capturing run metrics and social features, they miss the crucial supplemental work that becomes essential as athletes age - injury prevention routines, strength training consistency, and recovery practices. This web app combines automated data from Strava and Garmin APIs with streamlined manual logging for supplemental activities, creating a complete picture of training load and health patterns. 
The goal is to surface actionable insights through an on-demand AI coach rather than automated notifications, helping users make informed training decisions based on the full context of their recent activity.
Built with a mobile-first, dashboard-focused design inspired by professional tools like Stripe and Mercury, the app prioritizes clean data visualization over gamification or social features. Key metrics are prominently displayed alongside trend charts and training load analysis, while hard-coded red flags provide immediate feedback on potential issues like insufficient recovery or injury prevention gaps. 
The conversational AI coach leverages the complete training context to answer specific questions about patterns, workout planning, and recovery needs - making this a tool for athletes who understand training principles but want data-driven insights to optimize their approach for long-term consistency and health.
